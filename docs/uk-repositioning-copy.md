# 1. UK Market page — `/markets/united-kingdom`

**SEO title:** Offshore Accounting Team for UK Accountancy Practices
**Meta description:** Accounstone works as your practice's offshore accounting team — bookkeeping, year-end accounts and tax preparation under FRS 102, prepared for your review and your filing.

## H1: Your Practice's Offshore Accounting Team

**Lead paragraph**

Most UK practices do not have a talent problem. They have a capacity problem with
a seasonal shape: the VAT quarters keep arriving, January absorbs everything, and
the year-end accounts that were supposed to be done in September are still open in
February. Accounstone exists to take the preparation layer off that curve — so
your qualified people spend their hours reviewing and advising rather than
processing.

### H2: We Are Not a Second Practice. We Are Your Delivery Layer.

There is an important distinction here and it decides whether an arrangement like
this works. We do not hold your client relationships, we do not sign anything, and
we do not file. Your practice stays the client's accountant in every sense that
matters — professionally, contractually, and in the client's mind. What moves is
the preparation underneath: the posting, the reconciling, the schedule-building,
the workpapers.

That boundary is not a disclaimer. It is the reason the arrangement is safe to
enter. A supplier who blurs it is offering you a competitor with access to your
client list.

### H2: What Your Practice Hands Over, and What It Keeps

**Prepared by us**
- Transaction processing and coding across your client portfolio
- Bank, card, and control-account reconciliations
- VAT-quarter records kept in a state the return can be prepared from
- Year-end accounts preparation under FRS 102 or FRS 105
- Corporation Tax computations and CT600 workpapers
- Self Assessment workpapers and supporting schedules
- Payroll processing, RTI submission data, and auto-enrolment records
- Lead schedules and supporting evidence for audit engagements

**Kept by your practice**
- The client relationship, and every client-facing conversation
- Review, professional judgement, and sign-off
- Every submission to HMRC and Companies House
- Advice of any kind, including anything approaching tax planning
- Accounting policy decisions and any treatment genuinely open to argument

We do not hold HMRC or Companies House credentials. We do not want them.

### H2: Built Around the UK Compliance Calendar

Your year has a shape, and the work should be organised around it rather than
arriving as a flat monthly service.

- **VAT quarters.** Records reconciled and coded through the quarter, not
  reconstructed in the week the return is due.
- **PAYE, every pay date.** Real Time Information leaves no catch-up window, so
  the check happens before submission rather than at year end.
- **Auto-enrolment, every period.** Assessment runs each pay run, not once at
  onboarding.
- **Year-end accounts.** Disclosure inputs — related parties, commitments, leases
  — accumulated during the year rather than reconstructed under deadline.
- **Corporation Tax.** Computations built on an agreed set of accounts rather than
  a moving one.
- **31 January.** The volume is known in advance, which means the capacity for it
  can be too.

### H2: Making Tax Digital, Handled Properly

MTD is the requirement most often satisfied on paper and broken in practice. The
rule is a digital link from the underlying record to the submitted figure. The
break is rarely dramatic — someone exports to a spreadsheet, adjusts a total, and
types the result in. Keeping the work inside Xero, QuickBooks or Sage rather than
around it is most of what compliance actually requires, and it is how we work by
default.

### H2: The Platforms Your Clients Already Run

Xero and QuickBooks Online dominate the UK practice market, with Sage still common
in more established client books. We work inside whichever platform is already in
place, using your chart of accounts and your conventions. We do not implement,
configure, or migrate software — that is a separate discipline and not one we
offer.

### H2: How an Engagement Actually Runs

1. **Scoping call.** Which client segments, which platforms, what your review
   points are, and where the pressure actually sits.
2. **Pilot.** A defined block of work — often one client or one VAT quarter — so
   both sides can see the output before anything scales.
3. **Documented workflow.** Preparer, reviewer, due dates relative to your
   deadlines, and what happens when a client's records are incomplete.
4. **Steady state.** Recurring work delivered to the calendar, with a named point
   of contact and an open-items list you can see rather than ask for.
5. **Review.** Scope revisited as your portfolio changes.

### H2: Quality Review, Before It Reaches You

Every file has a named preparer and a named internal reviewer before it reaches
your practice. The internal review looks at exceptions and judgement items rather
than re-performing the posting. What arrives with you is finished work plus an
explicit list of open questions — not a folder to sort.

Where a client's records are incomplete, the item goes to an open-items list with
what is missing and who was asked. It does not receive a plausible code and
disappear into the ledger.

### H2: Data Security and Confidentiality

- NDA-backed engagements as standard
- Access limited to the team members working your portfolio
- Work performed inside your systems, under access you grant and can revoke
- No banking control, and no submission credentials, at any point
- Documented workflows and retained evidence for every step

We are actively working toward SOC 2. We do not hold it, and we will not describe
it as though we do.

### H2: Turnaround

Turnaround is agreed per workflow rather than promised as a headline number,
because it depends on volume, platform, and how complete the client's records are.
What we commit to is that the agreed turnaround is written down, visible, and that
you hear about a slip from us before you notice it.

### H2: A Named Point of Contact

One person who knows your practice, your templates, and your portfolio. Not a
ticket queue, and not a different name each month.

### H2: UK Services

- **[Accounting & Year-End](/services/accounting/united-kingdom)** — close, journals and FRS 102 accounts preparation

### H2: FAQs

**Will our clients know you are involved?**
That is your call and we follow it. Most practices present us as their offshore
delivery team; some do not mention us at all. We are never client-facing unless
you ask us to be, and we never contact your clients directly without your
instruction.

**Do you file anything with HMRC or Companies House?**
No. We do not hold credentials for either. We prepare; your practice reviews,
signs and files.

**Which reporting framework do you work to?**
FRS 102 for most entities and FRS 105 for micro-entities. Where a client reports
under IFRS or FRS 101, we follow the treatment your practice has set. We do not
make the framework decision.

**What if a client's records are a mess?**
Common, and it is scoped separately from recurring work. Bringing prior periods
into agreement is a different job from keeping the current one current, and
pricing it as though it were routine would misrepresent both.

**Can you work to our templates?**
Yes, and we prefer to. Introducing a parallel set of workpapers creates
reconciliation work rather than removing it.

**Do you take on CIS work?**
Yes — subcontractor records, deduction tracking, and the supporting detail behind
returns. Verification and submission stay with your practice.

**How do we start without committing the whole portfolio?**
A pilot on one client or one VAT quarter. If the output is not what you wanted,
you have lost a quarter of one client's preparation work rather than a season.

# 2. Bookkeeping Support (UK) — `/services/bookkeeping/united-kingdom`

**SEO title:** Outsourced Bookkeeping for UK Accountancy Practices
**Meta description:** Bookkeeping support for UK practices — transaction processing, reconciliations and MTD-compatible VAT records, prepared to your templates for your review.

## H1: Bookkeeping Support for UK Practices

**Lead**

Client bookkeeping is the work that expands to fill whatever time a practice has,
and it is almost never the work that earns the fee. We take the recurring layer —
posting, reconciling, chasing the missing invoice, keeping VAT coding right as it
happens — so your people are reviewing rather than processing.

### H2: When the VAT Quarter Turns Into a Reconstruction

The pattern is recognisable. The ledger drifts for two months, the quarter ends,
and someone spends a week getting it into a state the return can be prepared from.
The work is not hard. It is compressed, and it happens four times a year per
client whether or not anyone has capacity for it.

Under MTD the cost of that drift went up, because the ledger is no longer just an
internal record — it is the substantiation behind a figure that has been
submitted. Getting coding right in the week it happens is a different job from
getting it right in the week the return is due.

### H2: What We Prepare

- Transaction processing and coding to your chart of accounts
- Bank, credit card and control-account reconciliations
- VAT coding applied at entry — standard, zero-rated, exempt, outside the scope
- Reverse charge identified where it applies, including CIS and cross-border services
- MTD-compatible digital records with the digital link intact
- Purchase and sales ledger maintenance
- Month-end schedules and an open-items list
- Catch-up and cleanup work, scoped separately from the recurring engagement

### H2: What Decides Whether It Works

**Source documents are the real bottleneck.** A bank feed shows an amount and a
payee, not a VAT treatment. Without the invoice the recoverable amount cannot be
supported, and an unsupported claim is a different category of problem from a
miscoded one.

**The nominal ledger has to serve the year end too.** The same ledger that
produces the VAT return produces the year-end accounts and the CT computation. A
structure that satisfies the quarter but obscures accruals, prepayments or
director transactions creates work later that nobody budgeted for.

**Exceptions get raised, not guessed.** Where a transaction cannot be coded from
the feed alone, the choice is to guess or to ask. Guessing produces a clean-looking
ledger and a bad quarter.

### H2: Your Review Stays Yours

Your practice keeps VAT scheme decisions, any treatment genuinely open to
argument, the client conversation, and the submission. We do not hold HMRC
credentials.

### H2: Platforms

Xero, QuickBooks Online and Sage, inside your existing setup. We do not implement
or reconfigure them.

### H2: FAQs

**Can you work across our whole client book or just some clients?**
Either. Most practices start with a segment — often the highest-volume, lowest-margin
clients — and extend once the workflow is proven.

**How do you handle a client who sends everything in a carrier bag in month 11?**
By flagging it in month one. The open-items list makes a non-responsive client
visible to you early, while there is still time to have that conversation.

**Do you work to our file naming and templates?**
Yes.

# 3. Accounts & Tax Preparation Support (UK) — `/services/tax-preparation/united-kingdom`

**SEO title:** Corporation Tax & Self Assessment Preparation for UK Practices
**Meta description:** CT600 computations, Self Assessment workpapers and FRS 102 year-end accounts preparation for UK accountancy practices — prepared for your review and your filing.

## H1: Accounts & Tax Preparation Support for UK Practices

**Lead**

January is not a resourcing problem you can hire your way out of. The volume
arrives, it arrives at once, and the people who can review it are the same people
who could have prepared it. We prepare, so your reviewers only review.

### H2: When January Arrives and the Records Are Not Ready

The pressure is rarely the computation. It is that the records the computation
depends on have not been finished, so preparation begins with reconciliation.

A CT600 needs a settled set of accounts behind it. If the year end is still moving
while the computation is being built, the work gets done twice — once on the draft
position and again when the balances are agreed. The same is true of a Self
Assessment where income sources have not been confirmed complete.

### H2: What We Prepare

**Year-end accounts**
- Accounts preparation under FRS 102 and FRS 105
- Lead schedules, supporting workpapers and disclosure inputs
- Prior-year comparatives agreed and movements explained

**Corporation Tax**
- CT600 computations from an agreed set of accounts
- Capital allowances schedules and adjustments
- Supporting analysis for anything requiring your judgement

**Self Assessment**
- Workpapers from confirmed, complete income sources
- Property, employment, dividend and self-employment schedules
- A clear list of what is still outstanding from the client

### H2: The Handoff, Step by Step

**Intake.** Records gathered against a checklist matched to the return, so a CT600
is not started while the accounts are still open.
**Prepare.** Computations built from a reconciled position, with workpapers built
as the work proceeds rather than reconstructed afterwards.
**Flag.** Judgement items stopped and raised — an arguable disallowable, a
capital-versus-revenue question, a director transaction needing a decision.
**Review.** Your practitioner receives a prepared computation, tied-out
workpapers, and a short list of open questions.
**File.** Yours. Always.

### H2: The Boundary, Stated Plainly

We do not advise. Not on structure, not on timing, not on anything that would
constitute tax planning. We do not hold HMRC credentials and we do not submit. If
a question needs an opinion, it comes to you as a question with the supporting
detail attached — never as a decision already taken.

### H2: FAQs

**Can you handle volume in January specifically?**
That is the most common reason practices approach us. Capacity is agreed ahead of
the season rather than found during it.

**What if the bookkeeping underneath is not finished?**
Then tax preparation will not be quick regardless of who does it. Where that is
the situation, the bookkeeping catch-up is worth scoping first — it is the actual
bottleneck, and we would rather say so than take the work and disappoint you.

**Do you work in our tax software?**
Yes, inside your licences and your access.

# 4. Payroll Support (UK) — `/services/payroll/united-kingdom`

**SEO title:** Outsourced Payroll Support for UK Accountancy Practices
**Meta description:** PAYE processing, RTI submission data and auto-enrolment records for UK practices running client payroll — prepared and checked before you submit.

## H1: Payroll Support for UK Practices

**Lead**

Payroll is the service most practices offer, least enjoy, and can least afford to
get wrong. It runs on a fixed cadence with no catch-up window, and an error is
visible to the client in their own net pay.

### H2: RTI Removed the Room to Fix It Later

Before Real Time Information, a payroll error could be tidied up quietly before
year end. Now the Full Payment Submission goes to HMRC on or before each pay date,
so a mistake is reported at the moment it is made. Correcting it means an amended
submission with its own trail — and the employee has usually already seen the
number.

That changes which part of the process matters. The calculation is routine. The
check before submission is where the value sits.

### H2: What We Prepare

- Gross-to-net processing across your client payrolls
- Starters, leavers and mid-year tax code changes applied in-cycle
- RTI submission data prepared and checked before you submit
- Auto-enrolment assessment every pay period, not once at onboarding
- Pension contribution schedules and provider files
- Statutory payments — SSP, SMP, SPP
- P45s, P60s and year-end documentation
- Payroll journals posted into Xero, QuickBooks or Sage

### H2: What We Will Never Ask For

Banking control. We prepare the run and the payment file; releasing funds stays
with your practice or your client's authorised signatory. Any payroll arrangement
that requires handing over the ability to move money is worth questioning,
whoever is offering it.

Submission is yours too. We prepare the RTI data; your practice submits it.

### H2: Auto-Enrolment Is Easy to Miss

Eligibility has to be assessed each pay period. Someone crossing the earnings
threshold, or reaching the qualifying age, triggers duties that nothing external
announces. Assessment runs as part of the cycle rather than as an annual check.

### H2: FAQs

**Can you run payrolls for our clients under our brand?**
Yes. We are not client-facing unless you ask us to be.

**How do you handle a client who sends timesheets late, every month?**
The cut-off is agreed in the workflow and the open-items list makes lateness
visible to you before it becomes a late submission.

**Do you do CIS alongside payroll?**
We handle the accounting and record-keeping around CIS. Verification and
submission stay with your practice.

# 5. Dedicated Accounting Team — `/solutions/dedicated-accounting-teams`

**SEO title:** A Dedicated Offshore Accounting Team for Your UK Practice
**Meta description:** A named offshore team working only your portfolio, to your templates, your close calendar and your review points — with one point of contact.

## H1: A Dedicated Team, Not a Ticket Queue

**Lead**

There is a point at which ad-hoc capacity stops helping. Once the same questions
are being re-explained each month, the overhead of briefing outweighs the work
being handed over. That is the point to stop buying hours and start building a
team.

### H2: What "Dedicated" Actually Means Here

- **Named people**, working your portfolio and not rotating across accounts
- **Your templates and your chart of accounts**, not ours
- **Your close calendar**, with due dates set relative to your deadlines
- **Your review points**, documented, so nobody guesses where the boundary is
- **One point of contact** who knows your practice
- **Capacity that flexes** for the VAT quarter, the January peak and the year-end
  cycle, then narrows again — without rebuilding the process each time

### H2: How It Differs From Staff Augmentation

Staff augmentation adds hands to a process you are running. A dedicated team owns
defined workflows end to end, up to your review point. Augmentation suits a
temporary spike; a dedicated team suits recurring volume that is not going away.
Most practices start with the former and move to the latter once the workflow is
proven. See [Staff Augmentation](/solutions/staff-augmentation).

### H2: How the Team Is Built

1. **Portfolio review.** Client segments, platforms, volumes, and where the
   pressure actually sits.
2. **Workflow documentation.** Preparer, reviewer, due dates, escalation, and what
   happens when a client's records are incomplete.
3. **Pilot block.** One segment, run properly, so both sides can see the output.
4. **Team formed.** Named, trained on your templates, working your calendar.
5. **Quarterly review.** Scope and capacity revisited as the portfolio changes.

### H2: Quality Review, Before It Reaches You

Named preparer, named internal reviewer, exceptions and judgement items listed
explicitly. Your practice's review is the last one, not the only one.

### H2: FAQs

**How small can a dedicated team be?**
Small enough to be worth it. The threshold is recurring volume and repeated
briefing, not headcount.

**What happens if someone leaves?**
Documented workflows are the answer to that, which is why they exist from day one
rather than being written after the first handover goes badly.

**Can the team scale for January and shrink after?**
Yes — that flex is much of the point.
