# ICP: CPA Firms

Primary buyer. Page: `/industries/cpa-firms`. Reference: `AI-WEBSITE-GUIDE.md` "CPA Firms page" section, `AI-WEBSITE-GUIDE.md` Tier 1 content list.

## Who the buyer is

A partner or accounting-firm manager with limited review time who does not want another management problem. Secondary persona: an accounting firm manager thinking in terms of workflow (review queues, standardization, turnaround time) rather than abstract outsourcing.

## What they actually outsource

- Overflow bookkeeping and transaction-level preparation during tax season
- Client bookkeeping cleanup before a return can be prepared
- Tax-return workpaper preparation (source documents, schedules — see `knowledge/services/tax-preparation.md`)
- PBC documentation and audit-evidence organization (see `knowledge/services/audit-support.md`)
- AP/AR processing for firm-managed client books
- Staff augmentation during defined capacity crunches (tax season, an unfilled role, a project)

## Seasonal capacity pattern

Firms don't lack people overall — they lack capacity for a specific workflow at a specific time (tax season being the sharpest example). The constraint is usually senior staff time spent on work someone else could prepare, not raw headcount. This is the core insight the CPA-firms page and staff-augmentation content are built around.

## How the handoff works (the pattern used across the site)

1. Structured discovery — chart-of-accounts conventions, file naming, checklists, deadlines, reviewer preferences, recurring exceptions.
2. Scope and review points agreed before work starts.
3. Work delivered against the firm's own checklists/formatting standards (white-label where relevant).
4. Feedback from early files is documented and folded into the workflow so the same correction isn't repeated every cycle.

## Software workflows

- **CCH Axcess:** tax-practice review queues, document management, workpaper organization.
- **Drake Tax:** return-preparation capacity, source-document handling, tax-season volume.
- **QuickBooks Online / Xero:** the client-bookkeeping layer underneath the tax practice.

## Vocabulary they use

Review queue, busy season, realization, PBC list, workpapers, sign-off, chart-of-accounts conventions, white-label, ramp-up, handoff, exceptions, cleanup.

## Primary objection

"If I outsource this, will I spend more time reviewing it than doing it myself?" Every page targeting this ICP should answer this directly rather than assert quality. See `AI-WEBSITE-GUIDE.md` content psychology principles #1–#3.

## Adjacent trend: CAS (Client Accounting Services)

A growing number of CPA firms are building recurring CAS practices (ongoing bookkeeping + reporting as a monthly engagement, sometimes with advisory layered on top by the firm itself). Production capacity — not client demand — is usually the constraint on scaling CAS. See `/resources/guides/client-accounting-services-cas-guide` and `knowledge/services/accounting.md`. The delegation line is the same as everywhere else on the site: production work can be delegated, advisory/judgment/sign-off stays with the firm's licensed staff.

## Hard boundaries specific to this ICP

- Never imply Accounstone can sign, review, or take final responsibility for a client's return or audit file — see `knowledge/company/scope-boundaries.md` §2–3.
- Never claim IRS representation on behalf of the firm's clients.
- White-label language is fine (working within the firm's process/branding); it must not imply Accounstone becomes a licensed party to the engagement.
