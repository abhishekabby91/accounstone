# Market: Australia

Page: `/markets/australia`

## Regulatory context (what actually applies)

- **Accounting standard:** IFRS (via AASB — Australian Accounting Standards Board).
- **Tax authority:** ATO. GST and BAS (Business Activity Statement) reporting is the primary recurring compliance cycle.
- **Superannuation:** rules apply to payroll-related work (superannuation guarantee contributions) — payroll accounting scope only, not superannuation fund advice.
- **Regulator — do NOT cite:** ASIC (Australian Securities and Investments Commission) is primarily a financial-services/corporate-securities regulator, not an accounting/bookkeeping-service regulator. This was found live on the Compliance page and removed — see `knowledge/company/scope-boundaries.md` §6. The correct reference is **GST and BAS reporting standards**.
- **Lodgment:** income tax lodgment stays with a registered tax agent. BAS lodgment stays with a registered BAS agent. Accounstone must not hold ATO portal access.

## Terminology notes

- "BAS-ready" (not "sales tax filing") for GST/BAS preparation work.
- "Income tax return preparation" — not "lodgement." Lodgment implies the registered-agent filing act itself, which is out of scope.
- Do not use: "capital gains strategies," "negative gearing optimisation," "salary sacrificing" advice — these are tax-planning/advisory, not preparation. See `knowledge/company/scope-boundaries.md` §2.

## Buyer expectations

Australian buyers expect bookkeeping and BAS-ready records that plug into a registered tax/BAS agent's lodgment process, with MYOB alongside Xero and QuickBooks as common platforms.

## Software ecosystem

Xero, QuickBooks Online, MYOB (AU/NZ-specific).
