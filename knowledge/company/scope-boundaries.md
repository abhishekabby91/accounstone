# Scope Boundaries — What Accounstone Does NOT Do

**Read this before writing or approving any page, article, metadata description, or schema.**

Every boundary below was found as a live overclaim on the production site and removed. They are recorded here so they are not reintroduced. If a draft crosses one of these lines, the draft is wrong — not the boundary.

When a topic sits near a boundary, the correct pattern is: **describe the preparation work Accounstone does, then explicitly name who holds the judgment/authority.**

---

## 1. Financial services and advisory

**Not offered:** financial services, financial advisory, CFO services, wealth management, investment advice.

| Not allowed | Allowed |
|---|---|
| "financial services" | "accounting services", "bookkeeping services" |
| "financial advisory" | "accounting support" |
| "CFO-level guidance", "CFO Support" | "senior accounting lead", "accounting oversight" |
| "strategic financial guidance/partnership" | "dependable accounting support" |
| "Building Better Financial Futures" | "practical accounting support" |

**Terminology that IS fine** — these are accurate accounting deliverables, not service-category claims: financial statements, financial reporting, financial data, financial position, financial records.

**Historical note:** the site once had 10 internal links to `/services/cfo-support`, a page that never existed. Do not recreate it.

**Open question — do not resolve without the client:** a third-party directory lists "financial reporting" as an Accounstone *service line*. As an accounting deliverable this is fine. As a service line name it risks re-muddying this boundary. Awaiting decision.

## 2. Tax — preparation vs. advisory vs. representation

**Not offered:** tax planning, tax strategy, tax optimisation, tax reduction strategies, entity selection advice, IRS representation, Power of Attorney.

**IRS representation is a hard legal line.** Holding POA (Form 2848) requires being a licensed CPA, Enrolled Agent, or attorney under Circular 230. A page previously claimed "we can provide power of attorney to represent your interests in IRS matters." That was false as written and is the single highest-risk claim found on this site.

| Not allowed | Allowed |
|---|---|
| "IRS representation", "power of attorney" | "returns prepared for your CPA or EA to review and sign" |
| "tax planning", "tax strategy" | "tax return preparation", "workpaper preparation" |
| "tax optimisation", "tax reduction strategies" | "quarterly estimated tax calculations" |
| "capital gains strategies", "negative gearing optimisation", "salary sacrificing" (AU) | "BAS-ready GST records" |
| "multi-state tax optimisation" | "multi-state return preparation" |
| "income tax returns and lodgement" (AU) | "income tax return preparation" |

**Jurisdiction-specific authority holders:**
- **US** — final review, sign-off, e-filing, and any IRS contact: licensed CPA or Enrolled Agent
- **UK** — HMRC submission: the registered practitioner. Accounstone must not hold HMRC portal credentials.
- **AU** — lodgment: registered tax agent. BAS lodgment: registered BAS agent. Accounstone must not hold ATO portal access.

## 3. Audit — preparation vs. judgment

**Not offered:** audit opinions, audit planning, risk assessment, sampling decisions, evaluation of management representations, conclusions on account balances, signing audit reports.

**Allowed:** preparing PBC schedules, organising evidence, reconciliations, rollforwards, tying out workpapers, formatting the file for auditor review.

The distinction: *preparation work can be delegated; auditor judgment cannot.* This holds under US GAAS/PCAOB, UK FRC/ISAs, and Australian AUASB/ASAs alike.

---

## 4. HR

**Not offered:** HR compliance, benefits administration, employment law advice, regulatory filings, contractor-vs-employee classification.

**Allowed — payroll-related accounting only:** payroll processing, payroll tax withholding, payroll reporting, payroll GL reconciliation.

Broader HR functions belong to the client's HR provider or in-house team. Classification decisions belong to their employment attorney.

---

## 5. Software — support vs. implementation

**Not offered:** implementation, configuration, custom development, system administration, data migration tooling, or vendor training for any third-party platform (QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, Sage, CCH Axcess, Drake Tax, MYOB).

**Allowed:** the recurring accounting work *inside* an already-configured system.

Also not allowed: unverifiable superlatives about third-party products. "NetSuite is the leading cloud-based ERP" is a claim about Oracle's product that Accounstone cannot substantiate. Every `/technology/*` page should make the support-vs-implementation boundary explicit.

## 6. Regulators — verify applicability before naming

Before naming any regulator, confirm it actually regulates **accounting and bookkeeping service providers** in that jurisdiction — not the broader financial sector.

| Do not cite | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| FCA (UK) | Regulates financial services firms | AML requirements |
| ASIC (AU) | Financial services / corporate securities | GST and BAS reporting standards |

Both appeared on the Compliance page and directly contradicted the financial-services boundary above.

## 7. Absolute and unverifiable claims

Avoid: "full compliance", "full adherence", "guaranteed", "best", "number one", "world-class", "industry-leading", "unparalleled", "cheapest".

Avoid as empty filler: "seamless", "enterprise-grade", "cutting-edge", "state-of-the-art", "revolutionary", "deliver superior results".

Prefer: "structured around", "aligned with", "prepared to".

**Certifications:** Accounstone is *actively working toward* SOC 2. Do not describe it as held or achieved. Do not imply certifications the company does not have.

## 8. Industries and markets — claim only what exists

Only these six industries have dedicated pages and may be claimed as served: CPA Firms, Technology, Healthcare, E-Commerce, Real Estate, Professional Services.

Market pages previously listed Manufacturing, Construction, Mining, Agriculture, Hospitality and Non-profit with no supporting pages. Do not list an industry without a page behind it.

**Markets:** United States, United Kingdom, Australia.

**Open question — do not resolve without the client:** a third-party directory also lists Canada. The site has zero Canada content. Either the listing is wrong, or Canada is real and needs a full cluster. This is a factual claim about the business and must not be decided by an agent.

## The general test

> Would a regulator, a licensed CPA, or a client's attorney read this sentence and conclude Accounstone is claiming to do something it is not licensed or resourced to do?

If yes — or if you are unsure — describe the preparation work and name who holds the authority.
