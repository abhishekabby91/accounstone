# Service: Payroll Processing

Slug: `payroll` · Page: `/services/payroll`

## What it is

Payroll processing support: recurring calculations, employee records, payroll reports, and the accounting work around payroll (GL posting, withholding reconciliation). Workflow is built around the client's existing payroll provider and approval process — Accounstone is not the payroll provider/platform.

## Deliverables (can be delegated)

- Payroll processing and calculations
- Payroll tax withholding calculations
- Payroll reporting
- Payroll GL reconciliation
- Employee payroll records maintenance

## Explicitly NOT included (found and removed from production copy)

- HR compliance
- Benefits administration
- Employment law advice
- Regulatory filings (beyond payroll tax reporting itself)
- Contractor-vs-employee classification decisions

These belong to the client's HR provider, in-house HR team, or employment attorney. See `knowledge/company/scope-boundaries.md` §4.
