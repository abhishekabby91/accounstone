# Service: Accounts Receivable

Slug: `accounts-receivable` · Page: `/services/accounts-receivable`

## What it is

AR support covering invoicing, payment application, account reconciliation, aging review, and collections follow-up.

## Deliverables (can be delegated)

- Invoicing (based on client-defined terms/pricing)
- Cash application
- AR reconciliation
- Aging review and reporting
- Collections follow-up (routine, templated communication)

## Stays with the client

- Credit decisions (extending or revoking client credit)
- Write-off decisions
- Escalated collections/legal action
