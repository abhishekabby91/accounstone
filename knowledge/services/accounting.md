# Service: Accounting Services

Slug: `accounting` · Page: `/services/accounting`

## What it is

Day-to-day accounting work that sits between bookkeeping and reporting: reconciliations, close tasks, journal entries, account maintenance, and management reporting.

## Deliverables (can be delegated)

- Journal entries and account reconciliations
- Month-end and quarter-end close mechanics
- Management reporting (financial statements, supporting schedules)
- Account maintenance and general-ledger hygiene

## Stays with the client / CPA

- Interpreting the numbers for the business (advisory)
- Final review and sign-off on financial statements
- Any strategic or forecasting conversation with the client

## Authority boundary

"Financial reporting," "financial statements," and "financial data" are accurate descriptions of this work's deliverables. They are not a claim to offer "financial services" or "financial advisory" — see `knowledge/company/scope-boundaries.md` §1. This service does not include CFO-level guidance, forecasting, or strategic financial planning.
