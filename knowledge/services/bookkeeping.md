# Service: Bookkeeping

Slug: `bookkeeping` · Pages: `/services/bookkeeping`, `/services/bookkeeping/{united-states,united-kingdom,australia}`

## What it is

Recurring bookkeeping support: transaction posting, bank and credit-card reconciliations, chart-of-accounts maintenance, cleanup work, and the recurring tasks that feed month-end close.

## Deliverables (can be delegated)

- Transaction categorization and posting
- Bank and credit-card reconciliations
- Chart-of-accounts maintenance
- Catch-up / cleanup work on backlogged books (scoped separately from ongoing monthly work)
- Supporting schedules and documentation for month-end close
- Recurring monthly close tasks

## Stays with the client / CPA

- Final review and approval of the close
- Decisions about chart-of-accounts structure that affect tax or reporting strategy
- Any judgment call on how to categorize an ambiguous transaction where the client's intent matters
- Sign-off on financial statements

## Authority boundary

Bookkeeping is preparation work. It does not include tax planning, financial advisory, or forecasting. See `knowledge/company/scope-boundaries.md` §1 and §2.

## Platforms

QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage, NetSuite — support means working inside an existing setup, not implementing or configuring the platform.
