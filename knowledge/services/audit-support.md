# Service: Audit Support

Slug: `audit-support` · Pages: `/services/audit-support`, `/services/audit-support/{united-states,united-kingdom,australia}`

## What it is

Preparation work that helps an audit move faster: organizing schedules, reconciliations, supporting documents, evidence requests, and open items.

## Deliverables (can be delegated)

- PBC (Provided By Client) schedule preparation
- Organizing evidence and supporting documentation
- Reconciliations and rollforwards
- Tying out workpapers
- Formatting the file for auditor review
- Tracking open items/evidence requests

## Never included — hard boundary

- Audit opinions
- Audit planning or risk assessment
- Sampling decisions
- Evaluating management representations
- Conclusions on account balances
- Signing audit reports

The distinction: **preparation work can be delegated; auditor judgment cannot.** Holds under US GAAS/PCAOB, UK FRC/ISAs, and Australian AUASB/ASAs alike. See `knowledge/company/scope-boundaries.md` §3.
