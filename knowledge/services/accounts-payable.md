# Service: Accounts Payable

Slug: `accounts-payable` · Page: `/services/accounts-payable`

## What it is

AP support from invoice intake through coding, approval-tracking, payment preparation, vendor records, and reconciliation.

## Deliverables (can be delegated)

- Invoice intake and coding
- Approval-workflow tracking (routing to the client's approvers — Accounstone does not approve payments itself)
- Payment preparation (not execution, unless the client's own banking/approval process explicitly authorizes it)
- Vendor record maintenance
- AP reconciliation

## Stays with the client

- Final payment approval and authorization
- Vendor relationship decisions
- Fraud/exception judgment calls beyond flagging anomalies for review
